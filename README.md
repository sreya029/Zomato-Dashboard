# Zomato Restaurants Analysis (Interactive Dashboard creation using MS Excel)
## Project Objective 
You are hired as a consultant data analyst by zomato where the team is looking for expansion and opening restaurants. Your task is to come up with strategies/suggestions about opening newer restaurants.
## DataSet Used
- <a href="https://github.com/sreya029/Zomato-Dashboard/blob/main/Zomato_Data_final.xlsx">Data of Zomato Restaurant </a>
## Process
- Data Cleaning: Utilized functions like TRIM, Remove Duplicates and Missing values to ensure data accuracy.
- Data Enrichment: Enhanced the dataset with additional variables using XLOOKUP to cross-reference external data sources and to connect tables across workbook/s.
- Descriptive Analysis: Employed PivotTables for summarizing key metrics and identifying average order value across different regions and different cuisines.
- Visualization: Created dynamic charts and dashboards for data representation, enabling Interactive data exploration and for creating best story around the opening of restaurants.

Used data-driven insights to identify strategic opportunities for new restaurant openings. Enabled informed decision-making through dynamic visualizations and key performance metrics.
## Project Insight
- The ideal countries for opening new restaurants with lower competition are Indonesia, Philippines, New Zealand, and the United Arab Emirates, as they have fewer existing restaurants, reducing market saturation.
- These locations show high footfall per restaurant, indicating strong dining demand and potential customer engagement.
- The average cost for two is high, ensuring better revenue generation and profitability.
## Conclusion 
- Market Penetration: Identified top Countries for starting new restaurants and expansion opportunities.
- Customer Engagement: Analyzed customer taste and purchasing power. Advocating for improve ratings.
- Our in-depth analysis of Zomato's marketplace data unveils key opportunities for expanding its restaurant network, optimizing menu offerings, and enhancing customer dining experiences. These insights go beyond raw data—they serve as a roadmap for Zomato's next phase of growth, enabling strategic restaurant openings in high-demand areas, improving operational efficiency, and elevating partner and customer satisfaction.
